# Agent seats: three things that were not obvious (2026-11-25)

1. **City automation and auto-explore are client work, not engine rules.** `SetCityAutomation` and `SetAutomation { Explore }` only store intent; the app plans the build and the step at Ready (`app/src/automation.ts`, calling `city_automation_orders_json` and `explore_step`). A seat played by a program got no builds and a Scout that never moved until `legatum_agent::assist::ready_orders` ran the same helpers on each answer, offline and in the bridge. Any new client must do the same.
2. **Checking an order menu one order at a time is slow, and city orders cannot be batched.** Each `validate_orders` call builds the seat's view. Checking budget-free unit orders in rounds (one per unit) halved a late turn's menus (eight seats at turn 121: 605 ms to 282 ms) with identical output. Batching `SetProduction` gave wrong answers: one city's change frees or takes strategic supply another city's choice needs (a Forge "valid" in a batch was refused alone).
3. **The game ends when the resolved turn reaches the limit.** With `turn_limit` 15 the final state's `turn` is 16. Reports that call an end a turn-limit end must compare `state.turn - 1`, or a victory on the turn before the limit is misnamed.
